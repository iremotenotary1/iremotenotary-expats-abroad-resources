Privacy

Privacy notice

This page describes privacy practices for the static U.S. Documents for Americans & Expats Living Abroad Resource Center published by iRemoteNotary. It does not replace the organization-wide privacy policy.

What this site does

This property is a static educational website. It does not operate an account system, lead form, or payment flow on these pages. It does not intentionally collect names, passport numbers, document contents, or other personal customer data through forms on this microsite.

Third-party embeds and outbound links

A FeedWalls resource list may be embedded later from feedwalls.online. That third-party service may set cookies or collect technical data according to its own practices. Outbound links may send you to official government sites or pages on www.iremotenotary.com.

Server and hosting logs

When this site is later hosted on DigitalOcean App Platform (ondigitalocean.app), the hosting provider may retain standard access logs. Exact log retention depends on the final App Platform configuration, which is not assigned during local validation.

Organization privacy policy

For iRemoteNotary services and the main website, see the iRemoteNotary Privacy Policy.

Contact

Privacy questions about this microsite may be directed through the Contact page.
